# ノリノリREMIXサムネ風 画像生成プロンプト

## スタイル指定
```
rough indie anime style, grainy texture, hand-drawn feel,
imperfect sketchy lines, muted colors, lo-fi textured aesthetic
```

## 背景との統合
```
photo background color-graded to blend with anime character,
dappled sunlight, warm summer lighting
```
- 背景は実写ベースだが彩度・コントラストを抑えてキャラと馴染ませる

## 構図・ポーズ
- ダイナミックなアングル（ローアングル等）
- 表情は具体的に（「laughing with mouth open」「slight smile」等）
- 小道具との接触を明示（「hand on saddle」「holding helmet」等）

## NG指定
```
no text, no overlays, no fisheye distortion
```

## 使用例
```
A young woman with short messy dark hair and green eyes, crouching down
next to a folded blue Brompton bicycle on a paved mountain cycling path.
One hand resting on the bicycle saddle, the other holding a cycling helmet.
She is laughing with her mouth open, looking at the camera with genuine joy.
She wears a black and neon green cycling jersey. Dynamic composition with
slight low angle. The art style is rough indie anime - slightly grainy,
hand-drawn feel with imperfect sketchy lines, muted colors, lo-fi textured
aesthetic. The background is a well-maintained paved cycling path through
a forested mountain area with dappled sunlight through trees, color-graded
to blend with the anime character. No text, no overlays. Warm summer
daylight filtering through leaves.
```
